# Reference comparisons

All pages below were accessed on **26 October 2026**. The named references were inspected as live rendered pages at 1440×1000, not inferred from screenshots in the previous design document. Comparisons concern editorial mechanisms; they do not endorse the scientific claims of those publications. Screenshots are review evidence, not assets proposed for reuse.

Web research included searches for fitting Works in Progress science narratives, The Pudding's visual essays and explorable explanations. Five primary examples were retained. The optional Bencho library was not needed to evaluate this site; no dependency recommendation follows from these comparisons.

## Anthropic

Observed: the opening organizes title, paper link, a four-part contents rail and metadata around a restrained reading column. Selecting the caveats chapter moved into substantial limitations prose, with the current chapter identified above the article. The article develops its setup before discussing results. [Opening capture](evidence/reference-anthropic-opening-1440.jpg); [caveats capture](evidence/reference-anthropic-caveats-1440.jpg).

Implication: this site's adjacent result caveats are already a strength. Borrow the continuity between question, experiment and limitation—not simply a serif body or muted background. The local site's interactive opening is in fact more immediately evidentiary than this reference's video-led opening. That is worth retaining. This audit did not test the reference's video, mobile layout or accessibility conformance. [Primary page](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-faking).

## Swarmchasers

Observed: a compact opening puts attribution, an editorial correction and a plain-language account before technical detail. The contents disclosure expands into specific discovery and evidence topics. It includes a section explicitly declining to infer collaboration. [Opening](evidence/reference-swarmchase-opening-1440.jpg); [expanded contents](evidence/reference-swarmchase-contents-1440.jpg).

Implication: distinctiveness comes from a documented sequence of questions and findings, not unusual visual effects. The local process chapter currently offers more generic method summaries. It could instead show one interpretation that failed and what was tested next. Do not import this report's real-world stakes into a synthetic two-agent task. Its long contents also demonstrates a cost: an index can itself become a large reading obstacle. This audit opened the disclosure but did not test every nested section. [Primary report](https://swarmcha.se/posts/chinese-agent-fleet-full-report).

## Distill

Observed: the article embeds teaching examples inside its argument. In the cognitive-load section, pressing **Toggle Notation** reveals explanatory labels around a displayed equation while the equation stays present. The adjacent variables control offers a second, local layer. [Tested annotation state](evidence/reference-distill-notation-1440.jpg). Some opening gallery thumbnails were blank in this session, so the gallery was not treated as a robustness model.

Implication: the local opening follows a sound similar principle: add a record without losing the object being explained. Extend that principle to the desk by annotating the decisive event and identifying what each added record settles. Three global depths can remain, but they should not substitute for local explanation at difficult terms. This observation supports an editorial design choice; it is not evidence that either site improves learning outcomes. [Primary article](https://distill.pub/2020/communicating-with-interactive-articles/).

## Works in Progress

Observed: after the large illustration, a ruled title/byline/deck hierarchy gives way to a consistent reading column. The article moves from scientific disagreement and missing measurements to evidence and policy response. The live version also inserts a newsletter box early in the body. [Reading composition](evidence/reference-wip-story-1440.jpg).

Implication: build the local investigation from specific questions, evidence and changed interpretations. Its existing source contains enough material; no invented personal anecdote is necessary. Avoid taking “editorial” to mean a large decorative picture, and avoid adding this reference's interruption to a small research companion. The screenshot is a desktop composition check; footnote behavior and embedded charts were not tested. [Primary article](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/how-we-fixed-the-ozone-layer/).

## The Pudding

Observed: the essay defines uniformity, displays onion pieces, then gives controls affecting that same representation. I toggled the exploded view and changed the first cut slider from 10 to 9; the pieces separated and the displayed standard-deviation value changed from 37.3% to 48.3%. [Tested state](evidence/reference-pudding-exploded-1440.jpg). Its typography and onion imagery are tied to the subject. [Opening](evidence/reference-pudding-opening-1440.jpg).

Implication: the reader's action should have a legible consequence. Here the equivalent is not a decorative simulation: it is adding instructions or revision history and seeing exactly which inference changes. Preserve the real traces and explain why the conclusion changes. Do not make empirical results into adjustable toys or adopt the essay's exuberant style against the owner's brief. Mobile and assistive behavior were not evaluated. [Primary essay](https://pudding.cool/2025/08/onions/).

## What these comparisons justify

The common useful mechanism is **a stable explanatory object with well-timed context**. The current opening already has that object. The next editorial gain is to carry it through the investigation and evidence desk: one question, the relevant record, an interpretation, a counterexample, then the limit of the revised conclusion. None of these references establishes that the site needs more animation, 3D, shaders, a framework or a new typeface.
